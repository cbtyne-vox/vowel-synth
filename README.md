# Vowel Synth Lab

A browser-based vowel synthesizer for teaching acoustic registration (open, close and whoop timbres) in voice pedagogy, made by Chadley Ballantyne, Stetson University.

It shows harmonics and vocal-tract resonances on a piano keyboard, a grand staff, a spectrum and a waveform, so students can see H1 and H2 cross fR1 while they hear the timbre change.

- Source: Liljencrants-Fant glottal flow model, or the closure transient alone
- Five resonances, with fR1 tracking options (fixed, track H1, track H2) and a singer's formant cluster control
- Register (chest to head), decay, vibrato rate and extent
- Preloaded patterns, including the Bozeman scale (5 6 7 6 5 4 3 2 1)

Made in the tradition of MADDE, Svante Granqvist's formant synthesizer.

Open `index.html` in Chrome, or visit the live page through GitHub Pages.
